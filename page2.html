RULES AND REGULATIONS SHOULD BE FOLLOWED
1. Attendance & Time
Students should be punctual for school and assembly.
Classes must be attended regularly.
Permission is required to leave school early.

2. Uniform & Appearance
Students should wear the proper school uniform every day.
Grooming standards (e.g., hair, nails) are enforced.
No makeup or unapproved jewelry may be worn.

3. Conduct & Discipline
Respect teachers, staff, prefects, and fellow students.
Obscene language or rude behavior is not allowed.
Bullying, fighting, or unsafe behavior is prohibited. (Typical in most school rules, though not specific to this school’s publicly available page.)

4. Property, Mobile Phones & Electronics
Mobile phones and electronic devices are often not allowed.
Items found are confiscated (sometimes returned at the end of day or term).

5. School Activities
Students must participate in assemblies, sports, and clubs.
Educational environment must be respected. (in typical rules)

6. Safety & Rules During Movement
Permission is needed to leave school grounds.
Safety rules are followed on school transport.
